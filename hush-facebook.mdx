## **Features**
- 📺 Suggested and sponsored posts removed
- 🎬 No ads (including in Stories, Reel, Watch)
- 🎭 no tracking (ad telemetry and background ad downloads stop)
- ⬇️ Save videos from Stories, Reels and your feed
- 📺 Watch at the best quality Facebook streams
- 🛠️ Import & export settings
- 🔕 Block anoying notifications
- 🤖 Hide AI Slop content
- ⚙️ Settings Hushfacebook

<Callout icon="lightbulb" iconType="solid">
    How to access settings mod menu:<br />
    Long-press on the Hush Facebook icon on your home screen or<br />
    Open the app and tap and hold Facebook logo in the top-left corner
</Callout>

## **How to login**

You can login normally using your Facebook account
