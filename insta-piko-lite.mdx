## **Main Features**

- 📺 Disable Ads
- 🎞️ Disable Suggestion Content _(Suggested posts will still be shown)_
- ⛔ Disable Story from Home Feed
- ⛔ Disable Autoplay Video & Audio
- ⚙️ Hide navigation button
- 👍 Enable Follow back Indicator
- 👥 Friendship status indicator
- ⏭️ Disable Story Flipping
- 🔽 Download Media (Posts, Reels, Stories and Highlights)
- 👀 Unlimited replays on ephemeral media ( Unlimited replays on view once, view twice media before they get expired)
- 👻 Ghost Mode (View stories, View live, Typing status)
- 📱 Disable Screenshot Detection
- ⚙️ Piko Settings

<Tip>
  If you change settings in mod menu, please restart Insta Piko Lite by clearing from your recent app and open again
</Tip>

## **Build Info**

- Patching using CLI by Morphe and patches by Piko
- Apk base downloaded from APKMiror
- Package: <br />Default: `com.instagram.android`<br />Clone: `com.instagram.android.piko`
