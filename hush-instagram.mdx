## **Main features**
- 🎨 App icon (Default, Hushgram, IG Black, IG Plum)
- 🎞️ Disable Ads
- 📺 Feed (Hide suggested posts, sugested accounts, Threads posts, reels)
- 📷 Load full resolution photos
- 🔄 Rounded corner on posts
- 🚫 Hide Meta AI on search, feed, reels and profiles
- 👻 Ghost mode
- 🚫 Hide chat
- 💾 Download media (posts, reels, stories, cover, comments)
- 🎨 Customize story ring size and color
- ▶️ Playback quality (Video, Reels, Story)
- 🎨 Customize button share sheet
- 🖊️ Open comments without the keyboard
- 👥 Follow label
- 🎨 Customize Tab bar
- 🔕 Stop background work instagram for saving battery

<Tip>
  If you change settings in mod menu, please restart HushGram by clearing from your recent app and open again
</Tip>

## **Build info**
- Patching using CLI by Morphe and patches by HushGram
