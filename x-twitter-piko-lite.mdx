## Main features

- 🔄 Disable automatic timeline refresh
- 📺 Remove Ads, Google Ads
- 📈 Hide Promoted Trend
- ⤵️ Custom download folder
- 👥 Hide Recommended Users
- ☑️ Remove "Creators to subscribe" Banner
- 🔍️ Show sensitive media
- ⚙️ Settings Mod (Piko Settings)
- ↩️ Enable Undo Posts
- ⬇️ Download Video and Gif
- 🎨 Material you
- 🛠️ Customizatin Font, Navigation & Drawer

## About Lite features

- Splited ARM7 and ARM8

<Tip>
  All Features you can access to:

  Click Icon Profile (top left corner) > Settings & Support > Settings and Privacy > Scroll to bottom > Piko Mod Settings
</Tip>

## **How to Login**

Login normally by typing your username and password, don't use Google Button
