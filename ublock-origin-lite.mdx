## **Summary**

uBlock Origin Lite (uBOL) is open source browser extension for content filtering including ad blocking. uBOL built-in blocker of ads, trackers, crypto miners and more.<b />

uBOL is a good ad blocker for blocking ads in YouTube, because YouTube now showing more ads if you playing video on the web  And the saying goes in 2026:<b />
