## **Summary**

uBlock Origin Lite (uBOL) is open source browser extension for content filtering including ad blocking. uBOL built-in blocker of ads, trackers, crypto miners and more.<br />

uBOL is a good ad blocker for blocking ads in YouTube, because YouTube now showing more ads if you playing video on the web  And the saying goes in 2026:<br />
 "Where piracy sites have less ads then YouTube now"

## **Set uBOL**

Here how to set uBOL for best ad blocking for YouTube and for the websites you visit<br />
 _(in this tutorial i use Chrome)_

- First you have install uBOL for your Browser
    - <Icon icon="chrome"/> [Google Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ublock-origin-lite/ddkjiahejlhfcafbddmgiahcphecmpfh)
    - <Icon icon="edge"/> [Microsoft Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/ublock-origin-lite/cimighlppcgcoapaliogpjjdehbnofhn)
    - <Icon icon="firefox"/> [Mozilla Firefox](https://github.com/uBlockOrigin/uBOL-home/releases)
    - <Icon icon="safari"/> [Safari](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ublock-origin-lite/id6745342698)
- Open uBOL Dashboard by clicking icon gear (<Icon icon="gears"/>) uBOL on bar Chrome then click  Gear icon
- After Dashboard opened

        - [x] Checklist All Filters<br />
