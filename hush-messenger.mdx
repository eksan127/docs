## **Features**
- 🎬 No ads & Banner promotions
- 🎨 Unlock app icon
- 📌 Pin more chat
- 🤖 Hide AI (Floating, Sticker)
- 😊 Use system Emoji
- 📸 Send photos original quality
- 🎥 Send Video without re-encoding
- 🔄 Auto Replay
- 🕵️ Privacy (Stop analytics, Stop Ad attibution)
- 📸 Allow screenshot protect media
- 👻 Ghost Mode (Hide read receipts, Keep unsent message, View Stories anonymously)
- ⬇️ Save any story
- 🌐 Custom font

<Callout icon="lightbulb" iconType="solid">
    How to access settings mod menu:<br />
    Long-press on Messenger icon on your home screen or<br />
    Open the app and tap and hold Messenger logo in the top-left corner or<br />
    Hamburger menu on navigation bar.
</Callout>

## **How to Login**

It will automatically login using the account you use for Facebook
