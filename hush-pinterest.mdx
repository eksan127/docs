## **Features**
- 🎬 Hide ads
- 🤖 Hide AI labeled
- 🚫 Hide video pins
- 🚫 Disable analytics
- ⬇️ Download pins (Video, Image)
- ⬇️ Custom download folder
- 🎨 Customization interface (Hide create button, Hide search button, etc)
- ⚙️ HushPinterest settings
<Callout icon="lightbulb" iconType="solid">
    How to access settings mod menu:<br />
    Long-press on the Hush Pinterest icon on your home screen or<br />
    From three dots menu in profile page
</Callout>

## **How to login**
You can login normally using your Pinterest account
