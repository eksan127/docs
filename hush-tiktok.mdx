## **Features**
- ⚙️ Feed filters
- 🌙 Amoled dark mode
- 🚫 Remove ads
- 🚫 Hide AI-Generated videos
- 🎨 Customize Feed tabs & bottom tabs
- 📺 Video info (show publish date, change text size, time seconds, Country flag, etc.)
- 👉👆 Gesture controls (Swipe brightness, volume, seek, etc.)
- ▶️ Player (Progress bar, show length time video)
- ▶️ Keep playing videos in background (PiP)
- 📺 Video playback quality
- 🕒 Screen time reminders
- 🌐 Auto translate comments
- ⬇️ Download (videos, images, and comment sticker videos/images with sparated folders, without watermark, progress bar)
- 🛡️ Privacy (Lock TikTok, Stop tracking, ghost mode)
- 🎨 Custom font
- 🌐 Region (Spoof SIM and Country)
- ⚒️ Backup restore settings
